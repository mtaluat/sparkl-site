Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my tracking number?

It is on your booking confirmation message and on the label stuck on the parcel. Paste it into the tracking bar at the top of this page.

How do I know if you deliver to my area?

Use the Serviceability checker above and enter your 6-digit pincode. If your area is outside active last-mile range, bulk B2B freight drops may still be available.

Can my business integrate with Sparkl?

Yes. We work with enterprise clients through API-driven induction and live status events. Email enterprise@sparkllogistics.in and our team will get in touch.

How do I apply as a delivery executive?

Tap Apply as Executive in the careers section below and send us your details. Our team will contact you about the next steps.

Who do I contact for a delivery issue?

Write to cs@sparkllogistics.in with your tracking number and a short description, and our support team will follow up.
